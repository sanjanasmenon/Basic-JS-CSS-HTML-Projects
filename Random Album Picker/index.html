Random Album Generator

Ever look at your music collection and think to yourself, "wow. I'm in the mood to play an album, but I have no idea WHAT to play!" Well, you've come to the right place! Just upload a .csv file in the same format as this spreadsheet and generate an album!

PLEASE NOTE: Spreadsheet MUST include Artist name and Album title. In the spreadhseet they must be titled "Artist" and "Album". Adding the "Image" and "Type" parameters are optional, but more for quality of life. The randomizer will still work without it, it just won't look as pretty...
